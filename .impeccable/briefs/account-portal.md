# Account desktop

Mode: Operate. Scope: /login, /register, /cabinet and the shared Settings window.
Build path: code-led. User-pinned direction: old Mac desktop + Frutiger Aero;
no concept roll or raster UI comp needed for the explicitly specified composition.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Account linking lives in a usable desktop with two real apps: Account and
Settings. Preserve the existing routes and every authentication/linking state.

OWN-WORLD: Pearl menu chrome, Aqua traffic lights, glossy blue controls, sourced
Vista icons and Green Aero cursors over the supplied Asadal wallpaper. Smoked
blue Dark remains optional; Light is the fresh-install and invalid-storage default.

STORY: Open Account from the dock, sign in and link a Minecraft name. Open Settings
to choose Light, Dark or System. Closing a window never logs the person out.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 40px menu bar, an upper-centered account window with readable
75%+ white glass and a bottom dock. No floating tagline or duplicate scenery.
Window contents scroll inside the frame. Settings opens offset in front; mobile
stacks full-width windows above the dock. Wallpaper sits lower behind the desktop.

FORM: Explicit user-pinned desktop topology, no seed key. Signature interaction:
drag the title bar, resize from its grip, minimize/close and reopen from the dock;
keyboard arrows offer moving/resizing, with maximize/restore and a reset command.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: no fake map, trade, currency, telemetry or server facts. Keep sourced
artwork attribution, semantic controls, focus, reduced motion, clipboard fallback,
error feedback, link polling and persisted/cross-tab/OS theme synchronization.
